What's a stepEvery task heading (Superpowers "Task N", Spec Kit "T001", Kiro/OpenSpec task lists, or any H2 with a checklist) becomes a step. The Overview step holds goal, architecture and constraints.
What Photon checksFiles the task names (exist, new, or not found); identifiers it uses that nothing defines; tasks with code but no test; two tasks that touch one file; the spec's requirements with no task. All from the text and the repository; no AI needed.
Where the review goesApprove or Request changes sends your comments and verdict to the agent thread that produced the plan, or writes a .review.md next to it. Tasks you ticked stay ticked when the plan is revised, unless their text changed.
